Open finding · Operations Ask AI body handling

The actual handler consumed an entire 256 KiB body without Content-Length before denying a missing actor. An oversized question also consumed the complete body before schema rejection. The application has a field limit, but no streamed byte limit; the request deadline starts after parsing.

Native AI, used as a control, canceled the same sample after 28 KiB, the first 4 KiB chunk over its 24 KiB cap.

Next repair: bounded streaming input, authorization before input consumption where practical, and a deadline that covers parsing. Four observation assertions passed by recording these behaviors; they do not make this finding an acceptance pass.

Bounded local reproduction through actual handlers with mocked downstream transports. No deployed DoS, memory-exhaustion or hosting-limit claim. Product source has not been changed by this testing batch.
