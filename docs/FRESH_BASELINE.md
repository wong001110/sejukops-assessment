## Chosen boundary

Keep `supabase/migrations` as the immutable historical/Test track. The separate **post-retirement, schema-only** [baseline](../supabase/fresh/baseline.sql) was exported from the prepared Test application's `public` and `private` schemas after retirement of old assessment, quota, and anonymous-Demo RPC objects. It contains no Test users, credentials, business rows, or audit rows. Supabase-managed `auth`, `storage`, and `extensions` schemas must already exist on the target Supabase instance. A schema-only export does not include the two required workspace rows, their fictional branches, or the Guest AI policy. The reviewed [catalog seed](../supabase/fresh/catalog-seed.sql) creates these fixed rows using new workspace IDs; it must run after the baseline and before identity bootstrap. `scripts/p1-bootstrap-fresh.mjs` then creates fresh Demo/Owner identities. The historical `supabase/seed.sql` invokes the Demo-only business seed helper but cannot create the missing catalog rows.

This choice needs a separately maintained fresh migration track. Before any later schema migration is accepted, verify that both the prepared Test track and the fresh track arrive at the same application schema. Do not treat a schema dump as proof of equivalent grants, policies, or function behavior.
